Alex Mercer

Associate Professor · Institute of Mathematics, ETH Zürich

I am an Associate Professor at the Institute of Mathematics of ETH Zürich. My research is in geometric analysis and global differential geometry, with a particular focus on curvature constraints in Riemannian geometry and their topological consequences.

I am especially interested in questions relating the scalar curvature $\text{Ric} \geq 0$ to spectral geometry and index theory, and in the coarse geometry of non-compact manifolds.

Publications

Rigidity of stable minimal hypersurfaces via eigenvalue comparison

Alex Mercer, Sofia Lindqvist

Journal of Differential Geometry, 2024

We show that compact stable minimal hypersurfaces in manifolds with $\text{Ric} \geq (n-1)$ must be totally geodesic, via a spectral comparison on the stability operator.

Index theory and scalar curvature on spin manifolds

Alex Mercer

Advances in Mathematics, 2023

We extend the Lichnerowicz vanishing theorem to generalised Dirac operators on spin$^c$ manifolds, yielding new obstructions to positive scalar curvature.

Coarse geometry of Riemannian foliations with bounded geometry

Alex Mercer, David Kowalski, Yuki Tanaka

Geometry & Topology, 2022

We study the large-scale geometry of foliations via coarse index methods, proving a coarse analogue of the Connes fibration theorem.
